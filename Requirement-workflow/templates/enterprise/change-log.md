## Change Control Rules

- Before freeze, changes may be made in `requirements/working-draft/`.
- After freeze, changes require a new version and change reason.
- Do not edit frozen baselines in place.
- Add new decisions to the change log and update traceability.

**Why?** Enterprise requirement history needs auditability and rollback context.
