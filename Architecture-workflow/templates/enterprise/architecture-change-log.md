## Change Control Rules

- Before freeze, changes may be made in `architecture/working-draft/`.
- After freeze, changes require a new version and change reason.
- Do not edit frozen baselines in place.
- Update ADRs and traceability when design decisions change.

**Why?** Enterprise architecture needs a durable decision and change trail.
